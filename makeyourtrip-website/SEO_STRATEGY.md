# MakeYourTrip Inc. — 3-Tier SEO Keyword Strategy

This document records the planned keyword strategy for **MakeYourTrip Inc.**, categorized into three distinct tiers reflecting real search queries.

## Tier 1: Branding (Brand Identity & Disambiguation)
**Objective:** Establish brand dominance, build Knowledge Graph entity signals, and prevent search engines from autocorrecting queries to "MakeMyTrip".

| Target Keyword Phrase | Search Intent | Target Pages |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `makeyourtrip` | Branded Navigation | Homepage |
| `make your trip inc` | Exact Entity Match | Homepage, About |
| `makeyourtrip website` | Official Site Search | Homepage |
| `makeyourtrip travel` | Branded Industry Match | Homepage, Services |
| `make your trip agency` | Agency Brand Match | Homepage, About |
| `makeyourtrip reviews` | Reputation / Social Proof | Testimonials, About |
| `makeyourtrip contact number` | Direct Inquiry | Contact Page |
