# MakeYourTrip Inc. — Master Catalog of 36 Sub-Destinations

This catalog documents the complete 36 sub-destination pages planned for **MakeYourTrip Inc.**, organized by country and region.

---

## 🇯🇵 Japan (East Asia)
1. **Tokyo** — Neon cityscapes, Michelin-starred dining, modern culture, and bullet train hub.
2. **Kyoto** — Ancient shrines, traditional wooden ryokans, tea ceremonies, and bamboo groves.
3. **Osaka** — The culinary capital of Japan, vibrant Dotonbori nightlife, and historic castle grounds.
4. **Mount Fuji & Hakone** — Volcanic mountain views, relaxing onsen hot springs, and scenic cable cars.

---

## 🇮🇹 Italy (Europe)
5. **Rome** — Ancient Colosseum, Vatican City, historical monuments, and classic trattorias.
6. **Florence & Tuscany** — Renaissance masterpieces, rolling countryside, and world-class vineyards.
7. **Venice** — Iconic canal gondolas, grand palazzos, and St. Mark's Square.
8. **Amalfi Coast** — Cliffside towns (Positano, Ravello), Mediterranean views, and private boat charters.

---

## 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (Europe)
9. **London** — Historic royalty, West End theater shows, iconic museums, and Thames riverfront.
10. **Edinburgh** — Medieval Old Town, Edinburgh Castle, cobblestone wynds, and Scottish history.
11. **Scottish Highlands** — Dramatic lochs, Isle of Skye, Highland estates, and scenic driving routes.
12. **Bath & The Cotswolds** — Roman thermal baths, honey-colored stone cottages, and idyllic English countryside.

---

## 🇫🇷 France (Europe)
13. **Paris** — Eiffel Tower, the Louvre, world-class haute cuisine, fashion, and Seine cruises.
14. **French Riviera (Nice, Cannes & Monaco)** — Glamorous Mediterranean coastlines, yachting, and beach clubs.
15. **Provence** — Lavender fields, olive groves, quiet village markets, and historic vineyard estates.
16. **Bordeaux & Wine Country** — Renowned châteaux, wine tastings, and regional gastronomy.

---

## 🇨🇭 Switzerland (Europe)
17. **Zurich** — Lakeside elegance, historic luxury banking center, and upscale shopping.
18. **Lucerne** — Historic Chapel Bridge, Lake Lucerne paddle steamers, and Mount Pilatus excursions.
19. **Interlaken & Jungfraujoch** — Alpine adventure hub, dramatic valleys, and the "Top of Europe" glacier train.
20. **Zermatt (The Matterhorn)** — Car-free alpine village with dramatic views of the iconic peak.

---

## 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (Middle East)
21. **Dubai** — Burj Khalifa, luxury desert safaris, mega malls, palm island resorts, and VIP nightlife.
22. **Abu Dhabi** — Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Louvre Abu Dhabi, and private island retreats.

---

## 🇺🇸 United States (Americas)
23. **New York City** — Times Square, Broadway, Central Park, skyline observatories, and dining.
24. **California Coast (Los Angeles & San Francisco)** — Golden Gate Bridge, Hollywood, Pacific Coast Highway.
25. **Las Vegas** — The Strip, world-class entertainment residencies, dining, and luxury resort casinos.
26. **Grand Canyon & National Parks** — Dramatic canyon vistas, desert red rocks, and iconic road-trip views.

---

## 🇨🇦 Canada (Americas)
27. **Banff & Lake Louise** — Turquoise glacial lakes, Rocky Mountain peaks, and luxury alpine lodges.
28. **Vancouver** — Modern coastal city where towering mountains meet the Pacific Ocean.
29. **Toronto** — CN Tower, multicultural neighborhoods, and gateway to Niagara Falls.
30. **Montreal** — Historic Old Montreal, cobblestones, French-Canadian cuisine, and European flair.

---

## 🇬🇷 Greece (Europe)
31. **Athens** — Ancient Acropolis, Parthenon, historic Plaka neighborhood, and authentic tavernas.
32. **Santorini** — Whitewashed cliffside villas, blue domes, and world-famous caldera sunsets.
33. **Mykonos** — Vibrant beach clubs, chic Cycladic architecture, windmills, and Aegean nightlife.
34. **Crete** — Historic Minoan palaces, secluded lagoons, and authentic island cuisine.

---

## 🇸🇬 & 🇮🇩 Southeast Asia
35. **Singapore** — Marina Bay Sands, futuristic Gardens by the Bay, and world-renowned food centers.
36. **Bali (Ubud & Seminyak)** — Rainforest private pool villas, sacred temples, surf beaches, and wellness retreats.
